Delete Your SurveyCoin PK Account

At SurveyCoin PK, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your account and personal data.

Option 1: Request Immediate Account Deletion

To permanently delete your SurveyCoin PK account and all associated data, please send an email to:

support@surveycoinpk.com

Please use your registered email address and include the subject: "Delete My SurveyCoin PK Account".

Option 2: Automatic Account Removal

Accounts that remain inactive for 60 consecutive days may be permanently deleted along with their associated data.

What Will Be Deleted?

  • Your account information
  • Your profile details
  • Survey history
  • Coin balance
  • Withdrawal history
  • Activity records
Processing Time

Account deletion requests submitted by email are normally processed within 7 business days. Certain information may be retained where required by law or for fraud prevention purposes.
