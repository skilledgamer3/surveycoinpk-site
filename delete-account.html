Delete Your SurveyCoin PK Account

At SurveyCoin PK, we respect your privacy. You can request the permanent deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the SurveyCoin PK app.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your request.

If you are unable to access your account, you can also contact us by email:

support@surveycoinpk.com

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your account information
  • Profile details
  • Survey history
  • Coin balance
  • Withdrawal requests
  • Activity history
Processing Time:

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days. Some information may be retained only where required by applicable laws or for fraud prevention purposes.
